# Code of Conduct

This project builds learning software for children and the adults who teach them. The community around it should be something you'd be comfortable showing to the families who use it.

**Expected:** Be kind and assume good faith. Critique code and ideas, never people. Welcome newcomers and first-time contributors — a clumsy PR from someone who cares about kids' learning is worth ten drive-by dunks. Respect that maintainer time is finite.

**Not tolerated:** Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind; sexualized language or imagery; publishing others' private information; sustained disruption or bad-faith argument.

**Enforcement:** The maintainer may edit, lock, or remove contributions and comments, and may block contributors who violate this code. Serious or borderline situations: raise them privately via [GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/rowant-labs/classroompanel/security/advisories/new) or a direct message to the maintainer.

Keep it warm. It's a classroom.
