ClassroomPanel / Product North Star

The AI tutor with a living blackboard.

ClassroomPanel helps students learn by turning questions, notes, PDFs, and course material into one adaptive visual learning space. It does not just answer. It teaches, draws, simulates, quizzes, and adjusts.

The core idea

A student should be able to say, “I don’t understand derivatives,” and watch the panel become the lesson: graph, tangent line, explanation, animation, practice problem, and feedback — all on one board.

Conversational tutor Visual canvas Interactive simulations Adaptive checks

The answer machine

This is how most kids meet AI today: paste the homework, harvest the answer, move on. The research is blunt about where that leads — students who lean on it practice more and learn less. It feels like help. It is the opposite.

The tutor that makes them work

ClassroomPanel is built the other way. Before the board explains, your kid commits to a prediction. After it explains, they say it back in their own words, then take a quick check. The tutor never hands over the answer to graded work — it teaches toward your kid finding it. That difference is the whole product.

Your curriculum, not a feed

You choose the books. Upload the textbook, chapter, or syllabus your family is already using — even an 875-page textbook — and it becomes a living course: units, lessons, one board at a time. Progress is gated on demonstrated mastery, not on scrolling to the bottom, and concepts come back for review right when they’re about to fade. That spaced return trip is one of the strongest results in learning science, and it’s exactly what a one-off chat can never do.

Proof of work

Every prediction your kid commits to, every explanation in their own words, every quiz answered and concept mastered — and kept weeks later — is written into a learner record your family owns. Export it as a file. Bring it back. Move it to another computer, or another tutor entirely. It is evidence that the hours went in and the mastery came out — not a chat log that evaporates, and not a report card locked inside some company’s account system.

Mastery, concept by concept Spaced review, scheduled by the record Export it, own it, take it anywhere

Open source, parents in control

The entire learning product is open source (AGPL-3.0). You can read every rule the tutor follows — including the ones that refuse to dump answers — or have someone you trust audit them. Run it on your own machine if you want. For software your children learn from, “trust us” isn’t good enough; inspectable is the standard.

Your key, your data

No accounts. Nothing stored on anyone’s servers. Lessons run on a model API key you bring — you fund the AI directly and can cut it off any time. Your kid’s record lives in your browser and in the files you export. A built-in counselor watches for struggle and keeps you in the loop, because the adult in a kid’s learning should be a parent, not a platform.

What a session looks like

  1. Ask, or pick the next lesson from the course built out of your own curriculum.
  2. The board draws the lesson — explanation, live graph, diagram, simulation, or picture.
  3. Your kid does the work: predict first, say it back, then the quick check.
  4. The record remembers what stuck, what didn’t, and schedules the return visit.
  5. You stay in the loop — the counselor and the record show real progress, not screen time.
